Key findings

01
Solar is India's lowest-LCOE source.

Tariffs fell 69% from ₹6.47/kWh in 2010 to about ₹2 today, driven by competitive bidding.

02
Temperature drives voltage.

Voc falls about 0.29%/°C, so strings must be sized for the coldest site temperature to avoid inverter over-voltage.

03
Campus PR of 78.4% meets the benchmark.

The 70 kWp system yields ~1,24,320 kWh/yr and avoids 73.4 t CO₂ annually.

04
Preventive O&M catches faults early.

Quarterly thermography and Voc/Isc checks find hot-spots, diode faults and soiling before damage.

05
Policy supports the 500 GW 2030 goal.

ISTS waivers, TBCB transparency, ALMM mandates and RPO targets give investors certainty.
